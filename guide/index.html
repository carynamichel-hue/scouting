📋 What Scout Report is

A scouting form on the phone that sends every report to your own Google Sheet. A scout picks the farm and location, then for each pest taps a severity and a distribution. Many people can send to the same sheet at once, and each pest found becomes one row you can sort, filter and chart.

There is no account and no server of ours. The only "server" is a short script in your Google Sheet, so the reports and photos stay in your Google account.

1 · For scouts

  1. Open the link or scan the QR code your team shared. Tap Connect. The phone now sends to that sheet, with that sheet's lists.
  2. Put your name in once; the phone remembers it.
  3. Choose the Farm, then the Range if that farm is split into ranges, then the Location, and the crop if your team uses it. Farm and location stay filled in for your next stop.
  4. Choose a Pest, then tap its Severity and Distribution. + Add another pest adds more for the same stop. On a long list, just start typing a few letters of the name (“box” finds Boxwood blight and Boxtree moth) instead of scrolling.
  5. Found nothing? Tick Nothing found here. A clean check is worth recording too.
  6. Add notes, photos or your GPS location if your team has them turned on, then Send report. Farm and location stay filled in for the next stop.

No signal? The report is kept on the phone and sent by itself when signal comes back. The number on Report in the top bar is how many are waiting; Send now tries straight away.

Tip: add the page to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone, ⋮ → Install app on Android). It then opens like an app and works in the field.

2 · For the person who owns the sheet

Done once, on the Setup page (the ⚙ button at the top right, then Setup), which walks through each step:

  1. Make the sheet. A new Google Sheet, then Extensions → Apps Script. Paste the script, then turn on the settings file (Project Settings → “Show appsscript.json”) and paste the settings file over it (Setup has a copy button for each). Deploy as a Web app: Execute as: Me, Who has access: Anyone. “Anyone” is what lets scouts send without signing in to Google.
  2. Allow the permissions. Google warns “Google hasn’t verified this app”. It says that about every script someone writes for their own sheet, and the “developer” it names is you. Click Advanced → Go to (your project) (unsafe) → Allow. The settings file keeps the script’s reach to this one sheet and the photos folder it makes; it can’t see your other files, sheets or email, and it never deletes anything. If Google instead asks for “all your Google Drive files”, the settings file wasn’t saved: go back and paste it, then deploy again.
  3. Connect by pasting the Web app URL (it ends in /exec).
  4. Lists. Farms with their ranges and locations (one per line: Farm | Location, or Farm | Range | Location for a farm split into ranges), crops, pests, and the severity and distribution choices. Type them, paste from Excel, or upload an Excel or CSV file. Turn crop, photos, GPS and notes on or off.
  5. Save to the sheet with a setup password. The first save sets it. Scouts never need it; it stops anyone else changing the lists.
  6. Share the link or QR code with your scouts.

You can also edit the lists straight on the sheet's Setup tab. Phones pick up the change the next time they open the app.

3 · What lands in the sheet

  • Reports tab: one row per pest found, with when it was sent, the scout, farm, range, location, crop, pest, severity, distribution, notes, GPS and photo links. The rows of one visit share a Report ID; a report sent twice (a retry on weak signal) is only written once.
  • Scouting photos (your sheet’s name): a folder in your My Drive, made by the script the first time a photo arrives. Photos are shrunk on the phone first so they send on weak signal.
  • Setup tab: the lists and settings, one column per list.

4 · Good to know

  • Try it first with Try it without a sheet (demo) on the Report page. Demo reports stay on the phone and can be downloaded as a CSV.
  • Anyone holding the link can add reports and read the lists. Share it the way you would share the sheet itself.
  • Forgot the setup password? In Apps Script: Project Settings (gear) → Script properties → delete SETUP_KEY. The next save sets a new one.
  • Changed the script? Deploy → Manage deployments → edit → Version: New version. The link stays the same.
  • Reports waiting on a phone live only on that phone until they send. Don't clear the browser's data while the badge shows a number.
